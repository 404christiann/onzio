# Rose City FC captures

Public pages of https://rosecityfutbolclub.com/, a club site that runs on Onzio, captured on 2026-11-26 and 2026-11-27 in headless Chrome and compressed to WebP.

- `home-desktop-top.webp`: the homepage header and hero at 1280 px wide (page y 0 to 715).
- `home-desktop-rest.webp`: the homepage from "Next home match" through "2024 Champions" (page y 2411 to 5329). The sponsor raffle block between the two is left out.
- `home-phone.webp`: the homepage at 390 px wide, 2x (page y 0 to 381, then 897 to 2802).
- `schedule-desktop.webp`: the schedule page at 1280 px wide (page y 0 to 1470).
- `schedule-phone.webp`: the schedule page at 390 px wide, 2x (page y 0 to 1100).
- `roster-desktop.webp`, `roster-phone.webp`: the public roster page (page y 0 to 2300 at 1280 px; 0 to 2200 at 390 px).
- `news-desktop.webp`, `news-phone.webp`: the news page (page y 0 to 1180 at 1280 px; 0 to 1000 at 390 px).
- `crest.webp`: the club crest from the homepage header, resized to 192 px.

No private club data and no videos: the hero video appears only as the frame the capture caught.
